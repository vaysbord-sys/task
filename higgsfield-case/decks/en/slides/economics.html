What drives cost

  • Per usable shot: seconds × $/s × generations per keeper. Kalshi kept 15 of 300–400
  • Generation is 5–12% of cost; people and iteration are the rest. We sell keep-rate
  • Image-first routing cuts base generation from $960 to ~$240
  • Infra: shared cloud first; dedicated lanes for game-day SLAs; VPC only for residency

The objections

"Run open source on our GPUs." An 8×H100 server is $300–400K and idle on Tuesday. Three ML engineers ≈ $1.1M a year ≈ 2.75M seconds of Veo. Every model change is your migration.

"Price when GPUs drop?" They also rise: B200 rental +79% in 3 months. You pay per output; the rate steps down with list, re-routed quarterly.

The base case: 15 shots, 20 generations per keeper, 8 seconds billed, $0.40/s Veo Standard = $960 generation, plus $4.8K people and $2K music, VO and legal = about $7.8K. That is roughly 45x below the $354K 4A's average (2011 data). Against a $10–50K social spot the high case is not clearly cheaper, so for a sportsbook the ROI is the variant set: about $60 per extra state or channel version once hero shots are approved. Open-source answer: the best freely licensed open model trails Veo 3.1 by roughly 30–35 Elo; MiniMax H3's license excludes US, EU and UK use; self-hosted Wan 2.2 lands at about $0.09–0.14/s, the price of Veo Fast at lower quality. GPU answer: H100 rental fell about 64% from 2024 to mid-2025, then rose about 38% from October 2025 to March 2026; model prices fall faster than hardware (Veo Lite $0.05/s against $0.40 Standard).
