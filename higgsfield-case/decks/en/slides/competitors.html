Adobe wins on the enterprise agreement already signed and on governance: brand checks, Workfront approvals. Its partner-model indemnity covers GA Google and OpenAI models only, copyright only; Kling, Runway and Luma are excluded. Runway wins on the trust stack: SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 are published. Both aggregate third-party models: Firefly carries 30+, and Runway added Veo, Kling and Seedance in 2026. So I dropped "model breadth" as a claim after Part 4. The loudest complaint about both is paying for failed generations, which is exactly what outcome pricing fixes. Value wins the pilot; paperwork wins the contract.
