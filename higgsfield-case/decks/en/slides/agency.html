Sizing. About 20 producing creatives. Team plan: $395/mo for 5 seats and 5,000 credits ≈ $0.08 per credit. Four plans ≈ $19K a year before client volume (assumed 3x). Studio lands; Network is the 12-month target.

Land. $15K, 60-day paid pilot, 100% credited if the annual is signed within 30 days. KPIs: cost per delivered asset, turnaround hours, first-round approval.

Agency reality → answer

  • Bills per project → client sub-workspaces, usage export
  • Freelance headcount → unlimited seats (Runway Team caps at 9)
  • Bursty demand → pooled credits, 25% quarterly rollover
  • Iteration burn → credit-back on failures, image-first default
  • Client legal → SSO/SAML, RBAC, no-training, indemnity

Certified tier routes the ~100 inbound brand requests a day to agencies. They do the creative; Higgsfield stays the platform.

Priced on committed credits, i.e. output, not seats. Rates are order of magnitude; margin check per model with Finance

The metric is committed credits because that is what the agency rebills and what tracks our GPU cost. No seat fees because agency headcount swings with freelancers and seats punish exactly the usage we want. The agency's real worry is a surprise bill; the answer is a fixed annual commit, rollover and credit-back on technical failures. The certified tier formalizes demand Higgsfield already receives and today hands out informally: committed revenue plus distribution, and Higgsfield never becomes an agency.
