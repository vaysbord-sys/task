Five places Laza can take you.

An oasis, grandma's kitchen, the Turkish coast, an island, and pure sunshine. For each we researched where the feeling comes from, then let that shape the logo, the room, the uniform and the cup. Warm, cozy and generous first; cool second. Everything here was made for Laza.

Direction 01 · A desert oasis, and the inside of a Dubai chocolate bar

Oasis

Rest here.

A still pool under a round skylight, two date palms, and low oak benches around it. People sit close, the light is late-afternoon gold at any hour, and nobody rushes you.

Roots · research

  • Caravans stopped at oases to replenish water and food. Al-Ahsa, the world's largest, holds about 2.5 million date palms. source
  • Kunafa's origin is disputed (Palestine, Egypt, Syria). A common account places it in the 10th-century Fatimid court, and Nablus is the touchstone. source
  • Fix Dessert Chocolatier launched the pistachio-kunafa bar in Dubai in 2021. One TikTok passed 80M+ views, and the founder reports 30,000 orders in an hour. source

Arabic-contemporary lens · storytelling in the spirit of Mythology

What it means for Laza

Laza becomes the oasis on 5th Avenue: the 2am rest stop where water and something sweet arrive before you ask. The wordmark stretches like a kashida stroke, a caravan road. The symbol is the dallah, the coffee pot of welcome. The WOW moment is a counter built like a cross-section of the chocolate bar: layers you can see, a crunch you can hear.

Direction 02 · Grandma's kitchen, and a Turkish esnaf lokanta

Teta

Teta says: eat.

Indigo and ivory tile, a steam-table counter full of trays, open shelves of mismatched china, and long shared oak tables. It's loud in the best way.

Roots · research

  • 'Ahlan wa sahlan' joins ahl (family) and sahl (easy path): you are family, and may your way be easy. source
  • Hospitality (karam) is treated as a sacred obligation, rooted in the Bedouin ethic of caring for travelers. source
  • The esnaf lokantası is a tradesmen's canteen with a steam table of ready dishes, often family-run, with staff of 20 or more years. The principle is that you should feel at home. source

Brooklyn lens · human, neighborhood-proud, in the spirit of Red Antler

What it means for Laza

Teta (grandmother) feeds you before you ask, and the lokanta keeps the trays warm and visible. The brand is a name-tag, not a crest: a friendly Ruq'ah-cut wordmark with one diamond dot, and a round Turkish teapot for the symbol. The room is the tray wall: loud, warm, and tiled like a kitchen.

Direction 03 · The Turkish coastline, from Bodrum to Kaş

Aegean

Beyond the sea. Open till 2am.

Lime-white walls, ultramarine doors and shutters, and bougainvillea overhead. Cobalt glass lamps hang over long oak tables. It feels like a house in Bodrum, on a Queens street.

Roots · research

  • 'Ultramarine' is Middle Latin for 'beyond the sea'. The pigment came from lapis mined in Badakhshan, Afghanistan, and was once worth more than gold in Europe. source
  • In Bodrum, walls are whitewashed against insects and windows are painted blue against the evil eye, with bougainvillea over the doors. source
  • Maraş dondurma is stretchy ice cream made with salep and mastic. Its vendors turn serving into a playful street show. source

Northern-European lens · restraint in the spirit of Koto

What it means for Laza

The blue has come a long way, and so has Laza, from the Levant to Astoria. A heavy, calm, generously spaced wordmark with one crescent-cut counter. The symbol is a round maraş cone with a drooping scoop. The ritual is the stretch-and-flip at the ice-cream counter, modernized: striped linen, not a costume.

Direction 04 · A tropical island, on Brooklyn hours

Island

Island time. Brooklyn hours.

Blue tile, an oak deck-plank floor, rattan chairs, slow fans and banana plants. Friends lounge over mango desserts at 2pm, all year.

Roots · research

  • Tiki began in 1933–34 in Hollywood and Oakland as a fantasy tropics, and is now widely critiqued as cultural appropriation. So there are no masks, no totems and no pseudo-Polynesian type. source
  • Mango kunafa (kataifi, mango, cream, rose syrup) is an established modern twist that spread on TikTok and Instagram. source
  • Island time means slowness and sun. Laza's version is the unhurried 20-minute hang Moose asked for. (inference)

LA lens · sunny, social and content-first in the spirit of Media.Monks

What it means for Laza

Baby blue is the sky and the sea: the primary color, the one Moose asked for. Mango is the warm core that makes it sing. The escape comes from the fruit, the light and the pace, not a costume. A bubbly wordmark whose l curls like a Naskh stroke, and a round mango-kunafa symbol.

Direction 05 · Sunny yellow first, blue second: Laza's own La La Land

Yalla

Yalla. Good mood, open till 2am.

Sun-yellow walls and ceiling, a long cornflower-tiled counter with an oak top, and chrome stools. People of every age sit close. This is the La La Land feeling, built as a room.

Roots · research

  • Moose's emotional model is La La Land's yellow: one saturated color carrying the feeling across costume and set. Here the yellow is mango-warm, not lemon.
  • 'Yalla' is the everyday Arabic for 'come on, let's go'. It is used from Beirut to Brooklyn and is instantly social. (inference)
  • The ma'amoul, a round, stamped, shared cookie, is the holiday sweet of Levantine homes. It becomes the symbol. (inference)

New York lens · pop, social and joyful, a merge of Brooklyn warmth and LA energy

What it means for Laza

Yellow is the mood and blue is the promise Moose asked for. Together they're the happiest pairing on the color wheel: yellow-orange against blue. A round, thick, generously spaced wordmark with a floating dot and a flick. The room is sunlight you can walk into, day or 1am.
