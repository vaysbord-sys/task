Four directions, built from the ground up.

Every mark and photograph here was made for Laza. Each direction is a full system: a color scheme built on color theory, a type trio, a spacing scale and a voice. On your note, every blue sits next to oak and a brighter, warmer lead color. These are first iterations, shown before the full build.

Bespoke onlyNo stock imagery and no reused marks. Marks are drawn in Higgsfield vector; stills are art-directed per direction with no text or logos.

Blue is never aloneWherever blue appears, oak and a warm lead color (saffron, apricot or mango) come with it.

Contrast-checkedEvery text/ground pair is measured against WCAG. Colors under 4.5:1 are used for large display type only.

Copy from the categoryLines follow the patterns of the category leaders: short, sensory, specific, never "crafted with love".
