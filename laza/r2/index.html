The idea

Laza is the sun in a sky-blue day.

Moose asked for Laza's version of La La Land's yellow, delivered through a lighter, happier blue. We use both. The world is Laza Sky: walls, cups, uniforms. The brand itself is the sun inside it, a warm yellow wordmark that makes you smile when you see it.

01

Friendly firstChunky, soft, lowercase. It reads like a person saying hello, not a logo trying to be cool.

02

One Levantine gestureA single swash under the word, borrowed from the stretched stroke of Arabic calligraphy. That's the only ornament.

03

SoHo, not souvenirTwo colors, flat, confident. Premium but accessible, never gold and never kitsch.

Anatomy
  • Soft chunky lowercase: heavy, rounded, open counters. Legible at cup-stamp size.
  • The swash: the final a sweeps back under the word, a nod to the Arabic kashida. It underlines the name like a smile.
  • Two colors only: Night on Sun, or Sun on Night. No outlines, gradients or shadows.
  • Round 3: redraw as a production vector (optical kerning, swash taper, small-size cut without the swash).

Decisions for Moose

  1. Approve the swash wordmark as the direction.
  2. Confirm the name origin, لذّة ("delight"), and the Arabic spelling.
  3. Confirm the Sky + Sun split: the world is blue, the brand is the sun.

Round 3 build

  1. Production vector redraw of the wordmark, plus a small-size cut.
  2. Recolor symbol, pattern and badge into the system.
  3. Re-render storefront, cups, box and uniform with the real mark.
  4. Guidelines PDF and a kit of parts for store 20.

Caveats

  1. The wordmark is AI-drafted lettering. It needs a type-designer pass before trademark filing.
  2. A native reader must proof all Arabic.
  3. Renders show mood and direction, not architecture.
