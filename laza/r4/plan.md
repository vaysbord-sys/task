# LAZA · Round 4 · Creative plan

## 1. Moose's brief, extracted (from the email)
- **The job:** people come to *feel* Laza, not only for dessert. Mood lifts on entry.
- **Feeling words:** exciting, happy, playful, cozy, energized. Never boring, never over-sophisticated.
- **Emotional model:** La La Land's yellow, one color that carries the emotion.
- **Color:** a lighter, happier blue than today's royal blue. The studio chooses the final palette.
- **Materials:** oak (warmth of a beautiful home), chrome (current, youthful), tile welcome.
- **Social:** 20–30 minute hangs, people close together, music, movement. A day-club energy that isn't a club. "Feel like you're somewhere."
- **Photogenic:** 8–9/10, without trying. One major WOW moment on entry.
- **Price feel:** high-design but accessible. No gold, no marble, no luxury hotel. Not cheap, not childish.
- **Audience:** styled for 20–30, welcoming to families and older guests.
- **System:** cups, packaging, uniforms, menus, signage, phrases, music, furniture, social. One personality, recognizable without the logo, scalable to 20 stores.
- **Persona:** the person everyone wants to be around. Happy, confident, playful, social, not trying too hard. Makes you feel seen.

## 2. Round-4 rules from the client (29 Sep)
- Logos: thick, Arabic-inspired, whimsical, generously spaced. Never crowded. Wordmark in one color only.
- Symbol: one per direction, three colors, minimal at Nike/Adidas level, composed like the best marks.
- Every blue sits with oak and a brighter, warmer lead color.
- Everything bespoke. No generic imagery.
- Overall: warm, nice, cozy.

## 4. Logo system specs

**Wordmark (all directions):** lowercase "laza". Heavy weight with generous tracking, never touching. Each wordmark has one Arabic cue:
- **Oasis:** a kashida-style extended connecting stroke between the z and a, like calligraphy stretching. Color: Clay.
- **Teta:** soft Ruq'ah-like teardrop terminals and one diamond dot (nuqta) over the z. Color: Pomegranate.
- **Aegean:** a clean geometric heavy sans with one crescent-cut counter in the final a. Color: Ultramarine.
- **Island:** a rounded bubbly heavy form whose l ends in a Naskh-style curl. Color: Cornflower.

**Symbols (3 colors each, Nike-level minimal):**
- **Oasis:** dune + water + sun. Three stacked arcs forming a horizon (Clay, Saffron, Olive).
- **Teta:** a ma'amoul-mould flower of three petals around a center (Pomegranate, Saffron, Indigo).
- **Aegean:** a wave that curls into a scoop, with a bougainvillea petal (Ultramarine, Violet, Apricot).
- **Island:** a mango cheek, a leaf and a sun dot (Cornflower, Mango, Coral).
