Four places Laza can take you.

Each direction is a place people already feel something about: an oasis, grandma's kitchen, the Turkish coast, an island. We researched where each comes from and let that shape the logo, the room, the uniform and the cup. Warm, cozy and generous first; cool second. Everything shown was made for Laza.

DIRECTION 01 · A DESERT OASIS, AND THE INSIDE OF A DUBAI CHOCOLATE BAR

Oasis

Arabic-contemporary lens · storytelling in the spirit of Mythology · evolved from round 3 "Earthy"

Rest here.

Roots

Research, then the idea it gives us.

  • Caravans stopped at oases to replenish water and food; Al-Ahsa, the world's largest, holds about 2.5 million date palms. source
  • Kunafa's origin is disputed (Palestine, Egypt, Syria); a common account places it in the 10th-century Fatimid court. Nablus is the touchstone. source
  • Fix Dessert Chocolatier launched the pistachio-kunafa bar in Dubai in 2021; one TikTok passed 80M+ views and the founder reports 30,000 orders in an hour. source

Laza becomes the oasis on 5th Avenue: the 2am rest stop where water and something sweet arrive before you ask. The wordmark stretches like a kashida stroke, a caravan road. The symbol is dune, water and sun. The WOW is a counter built like a cross-section of the chocolate bar: layers you can see, a crunch you can hear.

DIRECTION 02 · GRANDMA'S KITCHEN, AND A TURKISH ESNAF LOKANTA

Teta

Brooklyn lens · human, neighborhood-proud, in the spirit of Red Antler · evolved from round 3 "Arabic"

Teta says: eat.

Roots

Research, then the idea it gives us.

  • 'Ahlan wa sahlan' joins ahl (family) and sahl (easy path): you are family, and may your way be easy. source
  • Hospitality (karam) is treated as a sacred obligation, rooted in the Bedouin ethic of caring for travelers. source
  • The esnaf lokantası is a tradesmen's canteen with a steam table of ready dishes, often family-run, with staff of 20 or more years. The principle is that you should feel at home. source

Teta (grandmother) feeds you before you ask, and the lokanta keeps the trays warm and visible. The brand is a name-tag, not a crest: a friendly Ruq'ah-cut wordmark with one diamond dot, and a three-petal ma'amoul flower pressed into plates and aprons. The room is the tray wall, loud and warm, tiled like a kitchen.

DIRECTION 03 · THE TURKISH COASTLINE, FROM BODRUM TO KAŞ

Aegean

Northern-European lens · restraint in the spirit of Koto · evolved from round 3 "Ultramarine + Violet"

Beyond the sea. Open till 2am.

Roots

Research, then the idea it gives us.

  • 'Ultramarine' is Middle Latin for 'beyond the sea'. The pigment came from lapis mined in Badakhshan, Afghanistan, and was once worth more than gold in Europe. source
  • In Bodrum, walls are whitewashed against insects and windows painted blue against the evil eye, with bougainvillea over the doors. source
  • Maraş dondurma is stretchy ice cream made with salep and mastic. Its vendors turn serving into a playful street show. source

The blue has come a long way, and Laza's has too, from the Levant to Astoria. A heavy, calm, generously spaced wordmark with one crescent-cut counter; a symbol where a wave curls into a scoop. The ritual is the stretch-and-flip at the ice-cream counter, modernized: striped linen, not a costume.

DIRECTION 04 · A TROPICAL ISLAND, ON BROOKLYN HOURS

Island

LA lens · sunny, social and content-first in the spirit of Media.Monks · evolved from round 3 "Blue"

Island time. Brooklyn hours.

Roots

Research, then the idea it gives us.

  • Tiki began in 1933–34 in Hollywood and Oakland as a fantasy tropics, and is now widely critiqued as cultural appropriation. So no masks, no totems, no pseudo-Polynesian type. source
  • Mango kunafa (kataifi, mango, cream, rose syrup) is an established modern twist, spread on TikTok and Instagram. source
  • Island time means slowness and sun. Laza's version is the unhurried 20-minute hang Moose asked for. (inference)

The escape is the fruit, the light and the pace, not a costume. A bubbly, heavy, well-spaced wordmark whose l curls like a Naskh stroke. A symbol of mango, leaf and sun. Oak decking and cornflower tile make the room feel like 2pm by the water, all year.
