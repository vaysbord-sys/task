# 07 — First 90 Days Plan (tailored to Magnific; transferable to Higgsfield)

A concrete operating plan you could present in a final interview or execute in your first weeks. It maps directly to the JD's responsibilities (global channel strategy, ecosystem from zero, tech partnerships, program design, co-sell/reseller motions, hiring US+EMEA, Salesforce attribution, pipeline/ROI). Adjust the specifics once you verify the company's live state.

> **Framing to open with:** "Magnific already has affiliate at the bottom and model/integration partnerships at the top, plus a direct enterprise motion with a €10M EU marketing fund. My mandate is the missing middle — a structured reseller/SI/agency channel and a repeatable co-sell motion — built *without* cannibalizing the 1M-subscriber self-serve engine. I'll prove one motion, instrument it in Salesforce, then scale and hire."

---

## Days 1–30 — Listen, baseline, design

**Learn & align (weeks 1–2)**
- Deep-dive the product, the credit/consumption pricing, and every existing program (affiliate, Creative Partners, AI Partners, API/enterprise). Map what exists so you build on it, not over it.
- Meet Sales, Product, Eng, Marketing, Legal, Finance, RevOps. Your **first deliverable is cross-functional alignment**, not a partner. Nail down: do direct reps get *credited* for partner-involved deals today? (If not, that's problem #1.)
- Get exec sponsorship and a written mandate: which revenue number partnerships owns, and the rules of engagement with direct sales.

**Design the foundation (weeks 3–4)**
- Write the **Ideal Partner Profile** for each type (creative/production studios, marketing agencies, resellers/VARs, SIs, technology/platform partners).
- Pick the **first motion to prove** — recommendation: **agency/studio reseller OR co-sell with one creative-tool platform** (where plugins already exist: Adobe, Figma, ComfyUI). One motion, not a portfolio.
- Draft program architecture: 3-tier structure, **consumption-based** commercial terms (credit margin + usage rev-share, not just acquisition bounties), MDF rules, and the deal-registration policy.
- Stand up **Salesforce attribution** with RevOps: partner Accounts, a deal-registration object, and **separate** sourced/influenced fields.

---

## Days 31–60 — Recruit the first wave, build the plumbing

- **Account-map** with Crossbeam against target segments to find warm paths; prioritize partners whose customers match Magnific's ICP.
- Recruit **5–8 first-wave partners** against the IPP (fit over volume). Sign them on the referral or reseller mockup from [05-contract-mockups.md](05-contract-mockups.md).
- Launch the **first co-marketing activity** and run a **deal-registration practice run** so the mechanics are proven before real deals.
- Begin **onboarding** (portal, agreements, kickoff, a 30/60/90 joint plan with each partner).
- Select/stand up the **PRM** (PartnerStack if payouts/referrals are day-one; Impartner/Allbound if enablement-heavy).
- Settle the **AI-specific integration stance** with Legal: a standard DPA + no-model-training default clause for technology partners.

---

## Days 61–90 — Produce proof

- **Target outputs:** first **partner-sourced registered deal**, first **co-selling conversation**, first **joint customer pitch**.
- Present a dated **joint business plan** with each first-wave partner at kickoff.
- Ship v1 of the **partner program guide** (tiers, benefits, deal-reg, RoE) and the **metrics dashboard** (sourced/influenced pipeline, activation rate, time-to-first-deal).
- Report to execs in their language: pipeline created, activation rate, and the ROI math — with a clear "invest / hold / sunset" recommendation per partner.

---

## Months 4–12 — Prove → scale → hire

- **Q2 — Prove:** drive first-deal **activation >50% within 90 days of signing**; run first **QBRs**; validate the consumption-based commercial model (watch credit breakage).
- **Q3 — Scale:** expand the first-wave; formalize **MDF/rebate/SPIFF**; launch **tiering**; tighten RoE based on real conflicts observed; begin a second motion (e.g., technology co-sell).
- **Q4 — Operationalize & staff:** hit a **partner-sourced pipeline target (20–30% of new pipeline** early-stage); run the renewal/re-tiering cycle; and execute the JD's hiring mandate — **hire the first regional Channel Partner Managers, starting US and EMEA**, once the motion is repeatable enough to hand off. Build the year-2 recruitment plan around **revenue-per-active-partner** (recruit more vs. grow existing).

---

## The commitments that make you sound senior

1. **One motion, proven, before scale.** "I won't build a sprawling program in month one — I'll prove one play produces partner-sourced pipeline, then templatize."
2. **Clean attribution from day one.** "Sourced and influenced in separate fields, registration-backed — so Finance and the CRO trust the number."
3. **No channel conflict by design.** "Direct reps get paid to support partner deals; deal-reg rules and RoE are written and enforced before I recruit."
4. **Consumption-aware economics.** "Partner commissions tied to usage, not just acquisition, because credits can expire unused."
5. **Integration before commercial.** "A strong plugin/integration is the entry ticket to co-marketing and co-sell — not the other way around."
6. **Hire against a proven motion.** "I bring in regional managers once there's a repeatable playbook for them to run, not before."
