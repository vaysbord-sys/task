## 4. The consumption/credit commercial model — what's different for partners

Credit/consumption pricing changes how you design partner economics. Instead of "% of a fixed subscription," margin attaches to **credits consumed / credit-pack resale**. Four structures you'd design [INFERENCE, grounded in SaaS norms]:
1. **Reseller credit margin** — partner buys credit packs wholesale, resells at list (20–40% band).
2. **Consumption revenue share** — ongoing % of credits a referred account actually *burns* (aligns partner incentive with usage, important when credits can expire unused).
3. **Referral/affiliate bounty** — fixed % of first subscription (the existing affiliate model).
4. **Co-sell/MDF** for enterprise (the €10M European fund is effectively market-development funding).

**Design tensions to raise in an interview:** who bears **unused/expired credits**; overage pricing and partner margin on overages; avoiding **channel conflict with the self-serve/PLG motion** that drives 1M+ subscribers; and that a floating credit value across image/video/audio makes "margin" harder to express than per-seat SaaS.

## 5. Interview-ready talking points

Use these to sound like you already do the job. Each pairs a fact with a point of view.

1. **"You've built affiliate at the bottom and model/integration partnerships at the top, but there's no structured reseller/SI/co-sell channel in between — that's the gap I'd build, without cannibalizing the self-serve engine."** (Shows you understand their actual state.)
2. **"Credit-based pricing means I'd tie partner commissions to *consumption*, not just acquisition, so partners are incentivized to drive real usage and reduce credit breakage."**
3. **"Your plugins in Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma and ComfyUI are the entry ticket to co-marketing and co-sell — a strong integration comes first, then the commercial motion, not the reverse."** (Echoes the tech-partnership principle.)
4. **"For an AI company, the make-or-break clause in any integration partnership is data-use and model-training rights — that's what enterprise partners scrutinize, and I'd standardize a DPA + no-model-training stance."**
5. **"I'd prove one motion first — likely agency/studio reseller or co-sell with one creative-tool platform — show partner-sourced pipeline, then templatize and hire regionally (US + EMEA)."** (Directly answers the JD's hiring mandate.)
6. **"I'd stand up clean Salesforce attribution from day one — sourced vs. influenced in *separate* fields, anchored on dated deal-registration records — because the fastest way to lose the CRO's trust is to report influenced as sourced."**
7. **On competition:** **"Krea is the funded all-in-one peer, Adobe is the enterprise incumbent with indemnification, Topaz is the fidelity point tool — our partner story is breadth of models + credit flexibility + the creative-quality heritage."**
