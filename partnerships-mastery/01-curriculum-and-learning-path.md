# 01 — Curriculum & 12-Week Learning Path

This is your structured on-ramp. It assumes ~5–7 focused hours/week. Each week has a **goal**, **inputs** (what to read/listen to — details in [02-resources](02-resources-people-podcasts-books.md)), and an **output** (something you produce, because you only learn this job by making the artifacts).

---

## The mental model first

Before the weeks, internalize the shape of the job. A channel/partnerships leader runs a **flywheel**, not a pipeline:

```
   IDENTIFY  ──►  RECRUIT  ──►  ONBOARD  ──►  ENABLE  ──►  ACTIVATE  ──►  GROW
   right-fit      sign the      make them     make them     first        QBRs,
   partners       partner       operational   competent     sourced deal  expand, re-tier
      ▲                                                                      │
      └──────────────────  sunset dead partners, reinvest  ◄────────────────┘
```

Everything you do maps to one of two questions:
1. **Am I recruiting the *right* partners?** (fit over volume — the #1 cause of dead programs is signing everyone)
2. **Am I moving partners from signed → *activated* (first real deal)?** (the enrollment→activation gap is where most programs leak)

Your scoreboard is **partner-sourced** and **partner-influenced** pipeline/revenue — kept as *separate* numbers, never blended.

## The 12 weeks

### Phase 1 — Why partnerships exist (strategy & vocabulary)

**Week 1 — The ecosystem thesis.**
- *Goal:* Understand *why* the channel shifted from reselling to ecosystems, and why buyers now trust "who" over "what."
- *Inputs:* Jay McBain content (LinkedIn + a Nearbound Podcast episode with him, e.g. "The Evolution of Business in the Decade of Ecosystems"); start Bob Moore's *Ecosystem-Led Growth*; subscribe to **ELG Insider** newsletter.
- *Output:* Write one page: "Why would Magnific (an AI creative platform with 1M+ self-serve subscribers) need a partner channel at all?" Force yourself to answer in revenue terms.

**Week 2 — The nearbound worldview.**
- *Goal:* Learn the "who economy" and the difference between inbound, outbound, and nearbound.
- *Inputs:* Jared Fuller's *Nearbound and the Rise of the Who Economy*; 2–3 Nearbound Podcast back-catalog episodes.
- *Output:* List the 7–10 types of partner that sit "near" an AI-creative buyer (e.g., design agencies, the buyer's existing tools like Adobe/Figma, AI model providers, SIs, creator communities).

### Phase 2 — How you build the program (operator playbook)

**Week 3 — The partner lifecycle.**
- *Goal:* Memorize recruit → onboard → enable → activate → grow, and the exit criteria of each stage.
- *Inputs:* Howdy Partners back catalog (esp. the internal/external enablement episode); [06-workflow-mockups.md](06-workflow-mockups.md) §1.
- *Output:* Draw the lifecycle from memory and annotate each stage with its one key metric.

**Week 4 — Partner types & motions.**
- *Goal:* Cleanly distinguish **referral vs. reseller vs. co-sell vs. tech/ISV** — different contracts, incentives, metrics.
- *Inputs:* [03-frameworks](03-frameworks-and-synthesized-knowledge.md) "Partner Motions"; [05-contract-mockups.md](05-contract-mockups.md).
- *Output:* A one-page decision tree: "Given this partner, which motion and which contract?"

**Week 5 — Program design: tiers, incentives, rules of engagement.**
- *Goal:* Be able to design a 3-tier program with thresholds, benefits, and conflict rules.
- *Inputs:* [06-workflow-mockups.md](06-workflow-mockups.md) §2; Crossbeam "Partner Tiers Cheat Sheet."
- *Output:* A tier table (Registered → Gold → Platinum) with entry criteria, margin, MDF, and benefits — for an AI creative platform specifically.

**Week 6 — Deal registration & channel conflict.**
- *Goal:* Understand the single most important operational control and why it makes or breaks partner trust.
- *Inputs:* [06-workflow-mockups.md](06-workflow-mockups.md) §5; Channeltivity/Zinfi deal-reg material.
- *Output:* Write your deal-registration policy: what qualifies, protection window, discount uplift, conflict resolution, who approves and in what SLA.

### Phase 3 — The hard skills (close, measure, align)

**Week 7 — Negotiation.**
- *Goal:* Replace positional haggling with tactical empathy; be able to run a partner-terms negotiation.
- *Inputs:* Chris Voss *Never Split the Difference* + Black Swan Group YouTube; Kwame Christian's *Negotiate Anything*; [03-frameworks](03-frameworks-and-synthesized-knowledge.md) negotiation playbook.
- *Output:* Script a mock negotiation: a reseller wants 40% margin, you can give 25%. Use labeling, calibrated questions, and an accusation audit.

**Week 8 — Salesforce attribution.**
- *Goal:* Model partners in a CRM and separate sourced vs. influenced defensibly.
- *Inputs:* [06-workflow-mockups.md](06-workflow-mockups.md) §3; the "counterfactual test."
- *Output:* Sketch the data model: partner Accounts, a deal-registration object, the Opportunity attribution fields, and the two reports you'd show a CRO.

**Week 9 — Metrics & the ROI case.**
- *Goal:* Speak the exec language — activation rate, sourced pipeline, revenue per active partner, program ROI.
- *Inputs:* [06-workflow-mockups.md](06-workflow-mockups.md) §4; SaaStr/GTMnow for exec framing.
- *Output:* Build a one-screen partner dashboard spec (the 6–8 metrics you'd report to the board).

### Phase 4 — Put it together (the company & the plan)

**Week 10 — The company & market.**
- *Goal:* Know Magnific/Higgsfield and the AI-creative competitive map cold.
- *Inputs:* [04-company-and-market-briefing.md](04-company-and-market-briefing.md); verify live pricing/programs on the company site.
- *Output:* A SWOT of the company's partner opportunity + a named list of 20 real target partners.

**Week 11 — Co-sell & technology partnerships.**
- *Goal:* Learn how to run co-sell with a platform partner and structure an integration partnership (critical for AI, where data/model-training clauses matter).
- *Inputs:* Inside Partnering / Ecosystem Aces; [05-contract-mockups.md](05-contract-mockups.md) §3–4.
- *Output:* A co-sell "rules of engagement" one-pager for a hypothetical Adobe or Figma partnership.

**Week 12 — Your 90-day plan.**
- *Goal:* Produce the plan you'd present in a final interview or your first week on the job.
- *Inputs:* [07-first-90-days-plan.md](07-first-90-days-plan.md).
- *Output:* Your own tailored 30/60/90, with named first-wave partners, the first motion you'd prove, and the metric you'd commit to.

## Fastest credible on-ramp (if you want to accelerate)

The strongest beginner bundle surfaced by the research:

1. **Read:** *Nearbound* (the why) → *Ecosystem-Led Growth* by Bob Moore (the how) → *Never Split the Difference* (before your first contract).
2. **Take one course:** impact.com's **Partnerships Experience Academy** (free fundamentals) to start; consider **PARTNERNOMICS SPLP** for a formal, exam-backed credential, or **Firneo**'s cohort for an intensive.
3. **Join one community:** **Partnership Leaders** (peer playbooks, benchmarking, the Catalyst conference). Many employers sponsor it.
4. **Subscribe:** **ELG Insider** (weekly) + follow **McBain, Fuller, Moore, Rowley** on LinkedIn.
5. **Listen weekly:** Howdy Partners (operator tactics) + 30 Minutes to President's Club (sales skills) + Negotiate Anything (negotiation).
