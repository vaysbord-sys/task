## A. Thought leaders & researchers to follow

### Tier 1 — know these cold

**Jay McBain** — Chief Analyst, Channels/Partnerships/Ecosystems at Canalys (now Omdia/Informa); formerly Forrester.
- The most-cited channel analyst alive. Frames the macro shift from reseller channels to multi-partner *ecosystems*.
- *Takeaway:* Up to ~75% of world trade flows indirectly, and buyers are influenced by many partners *before* a vendor engages — design for **influence** partners, not just transacting resellers.
- Follow on LinkedIn; Canalys/Omdia research.

**Bob Moore** — Co-founder & CEO, Crossbeam. Author, *Ecosystem-Led Growth* (Wiley, 2024).
- Coined/popularized **Ecosystem-Led Growth (ELG)** and built the leading account-mapping platform.
- *Takeaway:* Your partners' overlapping-customer data is your highest-signal GTM asset. Start every partnership by mapping account overlaps to find warm paths into target accounts.

**Jared Fuller** — Co-founder, nearbound.com. Author, *Nearbound and the Rise of the Who Economy* (foreword by McBain).
- The loudest evangelist of **nearbound** — surrounding the buyer with the partners/experts they already trust.
- *Takeaway:* Buyers shifted from "*how* do I solve this?" to "*who* can help me?" Position your company inside the trusted recommendations buyers already rely on.

**Jill Rowley** — GTM advisor; LP, Stage 2 Capital. Early Salesforce/Eloqua.
- Ties social selling to nearbound/partner-led motions; deeply credible with revenue leaders, not just partner teams.
- *Takeaway:* "Trust is the new data." Partnerships win because they transfer trust — engineer your motion around who the buyer already believes.

**Allan Adler** — Managing Partner, Digital Bridge Partners. Creator of the **GoToEcosystem** framework.
- The go-to consultant for *organizational alignment* around ecosystems (getting the whole C-suite ecosystem-first).
- *Takeaway:* Ecosystem is a **CEO function**, not a side team. If partnerships sit in a silo, the motion fails — treat cross-functional alignment as your first deliverable.

### Tier 2 — community & program authorities

- **Asher Mathew** — CEO, Partnership Leaders. Voice on the rise of the **Chief Partner Officer**. *Takeaway:* build with executive metrics (sourced/influenced revenue) from day one, not activity metrics.
- **Tai Rattigan** — Co-founder/COO, Partnership Leaders; ex-Global Head of Partnerships, Deel. *Takeaway:* partnerships scale when productized and self-serve for partners — don't let the motion stay bespoke.
- **Scott Pollack** — Founder, Firneo (cohort training/certification). *Takeaway:* partnerships has a learnable, repeatable playbook — invest in frameworks early rather than improvising.
- **Norma Watenpaugh** — Founding Principal, PhoenixCG; led US delegation for **ISO 44001** (collaborative business relationship management). *Takeaway:* strategic alliances need governance — joint value propositions, stakeholder alignment, an operating cadence — not just a signed agreement. (This is the "co-sell-with-hyperscalers" discipline.)
- **Mark Brigman** — Founder, PARTNERNOMICS; author. *Takeaway:* treat partnership management as a discipline with repeatable process and governance, not relationship charisma.

### Tier 3 — operators & platform founders

- **Mayank Bawa** — CEO, WorkSpan (enterprise co-sell/ecosystem platform used with hyperscalers). *Takeaway:* for AI/SaaS, the hyperscaler marketplaces are now a primary channel — master co-sell mechanics and marketplace private offers.
- **Greg Portnoy** — CEO, Euler; ex-Attentive. *Takeaway:* partnerships is **influence without authority** — your job is to get sales, product, and partners to act without controlling any of them.
- **Pete Caputa** — CEO, Databox; architect of HubSpot's agency partner program. *Takeaway:* a channel works when you make your partners money — teach them to sell and retain *their* clients before expecting them to resell you.
- **Rob Rebholz** — CEO, Superglue. *Takeaway:* co-sell scales only when you systematize partner-manager actions (templated plays, triggers on new account overlaps), not memory and goodwill.
- **Will Taylor, Ben Wright, Tom Burgess** — hosts of *Howdy Partners* (the most practical tactical show). *Takeaway:* focus partner time on revenue-generating activity; ruthlessly deprioritize partnerships with no clear deal path.

## C. Podcasts (ranked for your use)

**Status key:** ACTIVE = recent 2025–2026 episodes; ARCHIVE = high-value back catalog, few/no new episodes.

### Partnerships-specific
1. **The Nearbound Podcast** (formerly *PartnerUp*) — Jared Fuller & Isaac Morehouse. **ARCHIVE, essential.** The intellectual backbone of ELG/nearbound. Best episodes: the two Jay McBain episodes ("Decade of Ecosystems"; the partner-engagement one), and #148 on partner-program challenges.
2. **Howdy Partners** — Will Taylor, Ben Wright, Tom Burgess. **ARCHIVE (~76 eps).** The best *operator* tactics for a first-time partnerships leader. Standout: the internal/external enablement episode (#76) and the "fractional partner management" episode.
3. **Friends with Benefits** — Jason & Sam Yarborough. **ACTIVE.** The human/relational side — trust, internal politics, partner-as-friend dynamics.
4. **The Partnership Economy** (impact.com). **ACTIVE.** Breadth across affiliate/referral/B2B/B2C; brand-side case studies. (More consumer-weighted — mine for program-economics ideas.)
5. **The PARTNERNOMICS Show** — Dr. Mark Brigman. **ACTIVE.** Structured frameworks, governance, scorecards, vocabulary.
6. **Inside Partnering** + the **Ecosystem Aces** archive — Chip Rodgers (ex-WorkSpan). **ACTIVE / deep archive.** The best source on **enterprise co-sell with AWS/Microsoft/Google/SAP** — directly relevant to a global channel role.

### Sales & GTM (the skills layer under every partner-sourced deal)
7. **30 Minutes to President's Club** — Nick Cegelski & Armand Farrokh. **ACTIVE, top pick.** Hyper-actionable top-1% sales tactics (discovery, cold outreach, multithreading, objection handling). Companion book: *Cold Calling Sucks (And That's Why It Works)*.
8. **The Transaction** — Craig Rosenberg & Matt Amundson. **ACTIVE.** System-level modern B2B GTM — align partner motion with the core engine.
9. **The GTM Podcast** (GTMnow) — Scott Barker. **ACTIVE.** Org design, scaling, comp — relevant to partner-sales alignment.
10. **The Official SaaStr Podcast** — Jason Lemkin. **ACTIVE.** Exec/board altitude — how leaders evaluate the channel's ROI.
11. **Make It Happen Mondays** — John Barrows. **ACTIVE.** Sales fundamentals & leadership (the enablement layer for co-sell readiness). *(Also sample: The Sales Hacker Podcast, Predictable Revenue.)*

### Negotiation
12. **Negotiate Anything** — Kwame Christian. **ACTIVE, #1 negotiation podcast** (16M+ downloads). Negotiation, conflict resolution, difficult conversations — much of it internal/relational (your daily reality). Standout: "You Can't Change Them—But You Can Outsmart Them."
13. **Chris Voss / The Black Swan Group** — ex-FBI lead negotiator; author of *Never Split the Difference*. **ACTIVE (YouTube + book).** Tactical empathy: mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions, accusation audit — directly transferable to partner contract and revenue-split talks.

## E. Books (read in this order)

1. **Nearbound and the Rise of the Who Economy** — Jared Fuller (2024). *The "why."* Buyers trust *who*, not *what*.
2. **Ecosystem-Led Growth: A Blueprint for Sales and Marketing Success Using the Power of Partnerships** — **Bob Moore** (Wiley, 2024; ISBN 9781394226832). *The "how."* Partner overlap data → measurable pipeline. *(Note: this is Moore's book — not Fuller's or Rowley's.)*
3. **Never Split the Difference** — Chris Voss with Tahl Raz (2016). *Read before your first partner contract.* Tactical empathy over positional haggling.
4. **The Alliance: Managing Talent in the Networked Age** — Reid Hoffman, Ben Casnocha, Chris Yeh (2014). The canonical *alliance thinking* mental model: explicit, mutual "tours of duty."
5. **Partnernomics** / **LAUNCH** — Mark Brigman. The structured "science of partnering." **[verify edition/ISBN]**
6. **Strategic Alliance Management** — Tjemkes, Vos, Burgers (3rd ed., 2023). Academic depth once you design complex strategic alliances. **[verify]**
- Browse wider: **Omdia's "Top 55 Channel Ecosystem Books"** (May 2024) is an annotated canon.
