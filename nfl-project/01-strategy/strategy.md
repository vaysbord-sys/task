# NFL INTERNATIONAL — BRAZIL · Spec project

**Working campaign name:** O PRÓXIMO ("The Next One")
**Figma file:** "NFL"
**Target:** NFL, Senior Creative Director, Brand Experience (spec / unaffiliated)

---

## The one-sentence idea

The first Brazilian in the Pro Football Hall of Fame is a kid playing flag football on a Rio beach **right now** — and the NFL's job in Brazil isn't to sell a game, it's to hand the game to her.

## Why this is on-strategy (not a fantasy)

- **Real tentpole:** Ravens vs Cowboys, **Maracanã, Rio de Janeiro, Sun Sep 27, 2026.** NFL committed to a minimum of 3 games over 5 years in Rio.
- **Real market:** Brazil is the NFL's **#2 international market — 36M+ fans.**
- **Real grassroots already running:** NFL Flag Brazil w/ Samba Digital — **80 schools, PE coaches trained to NFL standard, boys+girls 12–15 pathway, 300+ youth in São Paulo, first U-15 girls' championship.**
- **Real precedent (NBA in Mexico):** games + Jr. NBA youth camps + a 200-coach clinic + "NBA House" pop-up + first official store + local leagues (Liga Telmex, NBA3X). The full stack, proven.
- **Real pioneer:** **Cairo Santos** — first Brazilian-born NFL player (São Paulo), played soccer/futsal before football. The soccer→football bridge in one person.

**The strategic spine — the flywheel:** a game = a *spike* (spectators, gone by Monday). Flag football = the *flywheel* (players → lifelong fans → the first homegrown Brazilian NFL star → a self-sustaining market). The game markets flag; flag markets the game. This is exactly what the NFL is already doing without a unifying creative idea on top — which is the gap this work fills.

## The audience truth

Brazil is the country of *jogo bonito* — the beautiful game, played barefoot on sand and in the street. American football looks like the opposite: armor, rules, stoppages. **The bridge is the body** — futsal footwork is route-running, capoeira is a juke, the beach is already a field. Flag football is the version of American football that Brazil can play *its* way: fast, skillful, no armor, on the sand.

## Design system

- **Ground:** near-black `#0A0B0D`
- **Commitment:** Brazil green `#00E676` + gold `#FFC400` accent (green-and-gold = Brazil *and* aggressive/edgy)
- **Type:** one condensed display grotesk (Anton / Archivo Expanded tier) + clean grotesk body; oversized tabular numerals as objects
- **Motion:** one easing curve `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; the signature moment = the Rio map chalked as a field, cities igniting
- **Imagery:** two registers — (a) intense studio cut-outs of NFL elements recolored to Brazil; (b) real/photographic Brazilian kids playing flag on the beach and in the favela. One grade across both.

## The 5-artboard architecture (maps 1:1 to the brief)

1. **NFL INTERNATIONAL** — opening. The shield goes global; Brazil lights up on the world map. Line + campaign name.
2. **BRAZIL** — the market case. Maracanã Sep 27, 36M fans, #2 market, the flywheel diagram.
3. **REFERENCES / CULTURE** — Brazil ↔ NFL: soccer/futsal/beach/Carnival culture, the soccer→football bridge, Cairo Santos + player connections. Well-organized reference wall.
4. **DECOMPOSING THE NFL** — the intense cut-out treatment: helmet, ball, jersey, goalpost, turf, referee, cleats, stadium — exploded onto the Rio map, then **recolored**: a new Brazil ball, a new Brazil jersey, and a mini Maracanã replica in the city.
5. **O PRÓXIMO** — Brazilian kids playing flag on Copacabana / in the favela. Graphic + real imagery. The grassroots payoff and the emotional close.
